# QuizMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

QuizMate is a student productivity app for managing subjects, quizzes, assignments, attendance, class schedules, date sheets, and reminders.

## Data We Collect

QuizMate may collect and store the following data when you use the app:

- Name
- Email address
- Registration number
- Semester, department, phone, and bio if added by you
- Profile picture if uploaded by you
- Subjects, tasks, quizzes, assignments, deadlines, and completion status
- Attendance settings and attendance records
- Class schedules, rooms, and date sheet details
- Notification and alert history

## Why We Collect Data

We use this data to provide the core app features:

- Create and manage your student account
- Save your subjects and academic tasks
- Track attendance and absence status
- Show upcoming classes, deadlines, and papers
- Send reminders and alerts
- Restore your data when you reinstall the app and log in again
- Verify your email address and help you reset your password through Firebase Authentication

## Data Sharing

QuizMate does not sell your personal data.

QuizMate uses Firebase Realtime Database to store app data. Contact links such as WhatsApp, Gmail, Instagram, and Facebook open only when you choose to contact support.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data. You should keep your password private and use a strong password.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account inside the app:

Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, subjects, tasks, attendance records, class schedules, date sheets, and saved alerts from QuizMate.

## Children

QuizMate is designed for students. It is not intended to collect data from children without appropriate permission.
